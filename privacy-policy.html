Privacy Policy

Bulb-u Mind Puzzle · Preet Studio · Effective date: 17 November 2026

In short: Bulb-u does not ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location. Your game progress is saved only on your phone. The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show those ads.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Bulb-u Mind Puzzle (the “App”), made by Preet Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat.

The App saves the following only on your device, so you can continue where you left off:

  • Levels unlocked and completed
  • Coins and unlocked hints
  • Settings such as music and sound on/off

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App can show rewarded video ads, for example when you choose to watch an ad to unlock a hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device’s Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • How you interact with ads (views, clicks)

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and user data

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation at any time in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).

4. Device permissions and sensors

  • Internet / network state — used only to load ads.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer) — some puzzles ask you to shake or turn your phone. Sensor readings are used live inside the game and are never stored or sent anywhere.

The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

5. Children

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not store any on our servers. Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google’s policies linked above.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
